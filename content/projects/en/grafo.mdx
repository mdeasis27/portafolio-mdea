## The problem

A relationship answer may require more than one passage.

**Who uses it:** Ontology analyst.

**The decision:** Answer from the graph or refuse as out of scope.

## The approach

Traverse committed nodes and edges, then compare the path with a single passage.

## Design decisions

Highlighted nodes and edges show the actual route beside the single-passage baseline.

Highlighted graph traversal beside a single-passage baseline; scoped to the local ontology.

## Current state

The primary experience runs locally without an API key or database. Scenario presets change actual inputs, and visual playback reveals computed trace events. Live integrations remain separate. The redesigned public routes are pending deployment.

## What this demonstrates

Makes every graph hop inspectable.
